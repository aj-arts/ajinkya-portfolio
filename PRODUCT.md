## Brand personality

Tactile, curious, independent. The user commissioned a full visual departure in September 2026 and explicitly asked for brave creative decisions, independent design reviews, screenshot iteration, and no visual references to the prior website. The personal instrument concept connects his engineering and music interests. See DESIGN.md for the new visual system.

## Anti-references

Generic AI portfolio templates, gradient headlines, dark neon terminals, glass cards, identical project tiles, generic serif magazine styling, and interactions that hide the actual work. Do not recreate a recognizable commercial music device. No stock photograph should pretend to depict Ajinkya.

## Design principles

1. Make the first screen recognizable even without reading its text.
2. Keep his name, software engineering focus, actual Microsoft internship title, résumé, and contact easy to find.
3. Give creative controls a useful job. The project sampler selects real work and links to it.
4. Let the work justify the claims. Use supplied project facts and screenshots, never invented outcomes.
5. Vary the page's rhythm. A bold opening leads to readable project stories, an expandable catalog, professional history, personal context, and contact.
6. Keep the whole portfolio useful without audio, animation, hover, or dragging.

## Accessibility and inclusion

Target WCAG AA contrast. Semantic headings and landmarks, keyboard-operable controls, visible focus, reduced-motion support, no autoplay audio, and responsive layouts without horizontal scrolling. Keep text readable and interactions clear. Sound is off by default and enabled only by a button press. External project links open in a new tab.
