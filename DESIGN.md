# Personal instrument

## Direction

A complete visual departure commissioned September 2026. Ajinkya's work in AI, security, research, and music becomes a custom project sampler. The hero is a physical composition, followed by spacious project stories and a compact catalog. It must remain an excellent engineering portfolio even with all animation disabled.

The user delegated creative decisions and asked for an autonomous complete redesign. Audience and factual content from PRODUCT.md remain useful; its earlier restrictions on accent coverage and illustration are superseded by this redesign.

## Scene and color

A hiring lead or creative engineering collaborator browses on a laptop in daylight, with enough curiosity to try a project key but little patience for hidden information. Use a bright vermilion stage, warm silver equipment, warm paper reading areas, and dark graphite ink. Acid yellow and lilac distinguish meaningful controls and projects. Color strategy: full palette. All CSS colors use OKLCH, with tinted neutrals.

## Typography

Voice: tactile, rhythmic, curious. Barlow Condensed in bold display weights evokes lettering printed on music equipment and concert posters. Chivo carries descriptions and controls. Avoid serif editorial conventions, fake terminal text, and decorative gradient typography.

## Layout and interaction

- The hero pairs an enormous two-line name with a silver sampler, at a slight angle on wide screens.
- Four real project keys select the record, project display, description, evidence, and outgoing link. Arrow buttons cycle the same selection. Sound is off until explicitly enabled.
- No dragging, hover, audio, or animation is required to access any content.
- Selected projects use large, varied compositions, real project images, concrete outcomes, and direct links.
- The remaining projects form a searchable-by-eye expandable catalog, with no generic card grid.
- Experience uses a readable chronology. Personal interests pair a lilac artwork with a quieter paper reading area. Contact ends in a full-color typographic composition.
- Skills and honors are always visible under static headings. Reading them must not require opening a disclosure.
- Avoid decorative asterisks and starburst marks. The user rejected their resemblance to Anthropic's symbol; award descriptions carry the meaning in plain text.

## Accessibility and motion

One h1, semantic sections, explicit link labels, visible focus, keyboard-accessible controls, 44px minimum controls, readable contrast, reduced-motion support. No scroll hijacking, cursor replacement, autoplay sound, or marquee. Content is visible without entrance-animation JavaScript. Short transforms animate only deliberate interactions. Mobile recomposes the instrument instead of clipping the desktop layout.

## Validation

Run production build and TypeScript checks. Inspect screenshots at desktop, tablet, and 390px mobile. Test project selection, keyboard navigation, disclosure rows, email copy, direct links, and reduced motion. Independent model review should evaluate screenshots and actual usability.

## Review decisions

Two image probes compared a physical instrument with a cut-paper curve composition. The instrument won. Independent Astra and Claude Desktop Fable 5.1 reviews informed the final refinements: project names on every key, real outcomes instead of a decorative waveform, larger display text, a compact mobile arrangement, fewer device labels, unobstructed poster captions, and quieter middle-page headings.
